Logic tables Says when a template fits

42 tables · 37 carry template hints

A table opens when the advisor’s words contain one of its trigger phrases. Working through its IF→THEN branches for that situation arrives at the templates that suit it, and each of those names is lifted in the ranking by +3.

The +3 is deliberately small — enough to settle a close call, never enough to overrule a strong match. measured 3 cases in 51 turned on logic tables alone.
