## The defect this exercise found — an apostrophe puts real tools on your queue

**`Porter's & Pine` is published. It is on your queue only because of the apostrophe.**

The name-matching function turns every non-letter into a space, so:

| | |
|---|---|
| The table writes | `Porter's & Pine` → normalises to `porter s pine` |
| The catalogue publishes | `Porters & Pine` → normalises to `porters pine` |
| Match? | **No** — the possessive splits the word in two |

**This is the same defect family as the digit bug found on 5 August**, where the scanner reported
*"Business Purchase Assessment"* because it stopped at the number, putting 9 rows on your queue that
needed no ruling and nearly withholding 8 correct sentences.

**Impact today is small but real: one false row.** The wider risk is not. The identical function
decides, at run time, whether a sentence naming a tool is withheld from the AI — so any apostrophe
name sitting in a gated field would be withheld even though the tool exists. Checked: today the
three apostrophe names sit in `action` and `notes`, which are not gated, so **nothing is being
wrongly withheld right now.** It is a trap rather than a live fault.

**Recommended fix:** strip apostrophes rather than replace them with a space — a one-line change in
`normalise` ([`server/utils/toolNameScan.js`](../server/utils/toolNameScan.js)). It can only ever
create matches, never withhold more. **Proposed separately; not done.**
