## ⚠ Two things to know before reading the rows

**1 · Four rows point at "Partner Accountability", and there are two documents with that name.**
The catalogue publishes one under *Do the Job* and one under *Get Organised*. The logic tables
store a template as **plain text**, so the name alone cannot tell them apart — and the screen's
picker offers titles only. On 5 August the de-merit one (*Do the Job*) was chosen. The rows below
will record the title; **which of the two documents an advisor actually lands on is not settled by
this file** and is noted as an open point at the end.

**2 · Nothing here reaches an advisor until a separate, reviewed step.** Recording a ruling does
not edit a logic table. The screen's "Apply it" button prepares a change and stops; a developer
commits the edit afterwards. That was the ruling of 2026-08-09 and it still holds.
