## 1. Why the push idea was written down verbatim

The "accept and push" idea was raised while testing the newly built page, and the design document
that holds it opens by explaining why it exists at all:

> *a design discussed and agreed in chat, and never written to a file, survives one day as a
> paraphrase and then not at all.*

So **the conversation is recorded verbatim first, and the analysis after it** — the original can
always be read back rather than somebody's later summary of it.

---

## 2. The finding that stopped it being one button

The owner's idea was to push a suggestion into whichever section it belonged to — distinctions,
logic table, or domain support — with one click.

**The reply, and the reason it was not built that way: the three are not equal.** One is a
genuine one-click — attaching an existing template to an existing row is a small, reversible,
fully-specified change. The others are not, because they would mean writing content nobody
authored, which runs straight into the rule that this app never invents the firm's material.

**Recorded because the idea was good and the uniform version of it would have been wrong.**
