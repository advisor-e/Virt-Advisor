Your Coach says

Five years of accounts show earnings growing from $149,921 to $571,795 once the owner's package is normalised — average actual growth 53.7%, though the 2024 dip (−21.6%) shows it is not a straight line. On your projected growth and discount rates, the business supports an enterprise value of $4,420,963.
