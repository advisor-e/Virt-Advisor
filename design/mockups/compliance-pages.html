Both halves stand: the declaration gates, the completeness check never does.
RULED 2026-09-10 — the tab is called "Compliance" Approved by Mike. Privacy & Compliance was rejected as accurate today and wrong the moment anything non-privacy is published here; Legal as reading like an offer of legal advice, which is the one impression this page must never give. Pinned — not to be reworded.
RULED 2026-09-10 — question 2 is answered: an update NOTIFIES, it never suspends Asked whether publishing an update should close the recorder until each firm re-declares, Mike ruled: "no, I publish an update - they get a notification such as a red dot next to the topic", then "we could also add the blue and orange - never opened and not opened in 3 weeks". Recorded here so it is not re-raised. RULED 2026-09-10 — the declaration, in Mike's own words 🔴 WRITTEN BY MIKE, VERBATIM. This string is load-bearing and is pinned — it is the sentence a firm manager is held to, and no session rewords it, tidies it or "improves" it:

"I confirm I have read the material provided above, and have read and understand the law as it applies to this firm in the country in which we operate. I act for and on behalf of my firm when I confirm we accept that meeting our legal obligations is our responsibility."

It replaced a proposal of ours and improved on it in two ways worth recording, because they are why it reads as it does. It is first person — the individual attests, rather than a firm attesting through nobody in particular. And it carries an authority clause, "I act for and on behalf of my firm", so the person ticking states they can bind the firm. Neither was in the draft. It still says nothing about legal advice, and never claims the firm is compliant.
RULED 2026-09-10 — a firm may never edit what a tier above it published Mike's words: "never edit ours." They read it, and they add their own material alongside it; the published text itself is not theirs to change. This is a deliberate departure from every other cascading block in this app, which offers accept / edit / switch off / add — so a build that reaches for the usual overlay shape will offer an edit control by habit. It must not.

The reason, recorded so it is not re-argued: a firm that edits our assessment and later relies on the edited version has our name on words we did not write, and the record no longer shows what we actually told them. That is the exposure the all-care basis exists to avoid, and it costs a firm nothing — they may add anything they like beside it.
RULED 2026-09-10 — nor may a firm HIDE a published item Asked separately from editing, because they are separate mechanics and the usual overlay offers both: Mike ruled a firm cannot switch off an item published to it. A published item is always visible at every tier below the one that published it.

🔴 AND THE DOT IS THE REASON, WHICH IS WHY IT HAD TO BE ASKED. Hiding an item would clear its red dot — the notification would defeat itself, and a firm could silently switch off the one signal telling them to read something new. A firm's OWN uploads stay theirs to remove. Only what arrives from above is fixed.
RULED 2026-09-10 — the completeness check runs on a button, never automatically Not on upload, and not on opening the page. The header states when it was last run and the firm asks for a fresh one.

⚠ ITEM 4.82 IS THE REASON AND IT IS STILL OPEN. Nothing anywhere caps how many paid AI readings a user can trigger; an automatic check on every upload would add a second route into that same unbilled hole. A build that "helpfully" re-checks on page load has undone this ruling without noticing.
