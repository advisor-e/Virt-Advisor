The decisions

Settled by the original — no decision, and no purple

Where the mark goes, what size, the footer line, the page number, the typography, the cover's composition and the navy are all the original's. The second draft recoloured the cover to the firm's colour and that has been reverted — it is Decision A below, and it is not ruled.

Decision A — does the firm's colour go any further than the logo

Mike asked for the firm's colours as well as the logo: “make it complete and get it all sorted once and for all.” Set against that, his 2026-09-18 ruling is that a client's document shows the advisor's brand and never Advisor-e's — and navy, blue and cyan are Advisor-e's palette. The two pull in opposite directions and only he can settle it.

THE ORIGINAL — NAVY
The cover as approved. Firm appears as its logo alone. This is what the drawing above shows.
THE FIRM'S COLOUR
The cover takes the firm's own colour. Consistent with the 33 concept drawings, which already do.

Recommended: keep the navy. It is the approved layout, every other document in the app uses it, and the firm is already unmistakably present as its logo on the cover and every footer. The 33 drawings take the firm's colour because they replaced his deck pages, which is a different problem.

Keep the original navy on the cover and dividers?

Decision B — one route, no screen, ship on the name alone

firmBrand() is built but on no route, which is why nothing binds. Its query is SELECT id, name always and only adds the logo and colour columns when configured — so the firm's real name works today.

Recommended: one Restify route; no screen, because the values live on Advisor-e's firm profile page (his 2026-08-15 ruling). Ship on the name alone — the words “Firm logo” leave both documents on day one and the logo arrives later with nothing more to build.

One backend route, no new screen, shipped without waiting for Advisor-e?

Decision C — the Business Performance Report in the same change

Same placeholder, same boxes, reserved by its own drawing in 2026. Leaving it means one client document branded and the other captioned.

Recommended: yes, both — the same three values into boxes that already exist, with no layout change to the report at all.

Fill the report's fourteen footers and its cover too?
