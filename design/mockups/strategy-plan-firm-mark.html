The decisions

Settled by his own page — no decision needed

The border's colour, thickness and inset; the interruption at the foot; both logo positions and sizes; every type size and colour. All measured from Advance.6.Organisational Review.pdf. Nothing here was chosen by us.

Decision A — a teaching page must not carry two frames

The 33 concept drawings are whole deck pages and already carry this exact frame and mark. Draw another around the sheet and a client sees two coloured frames millimetres apart.

Recommended: on a teaching page the drawing's own frame is the page's frame, and the sheet adds nothing. Every other sheet draws it. The teaching page above shows this.

One frame per sheet, the teaching page's coming from the drawing inside it?

Decision B — one route, no screen, ship on the firm's name alone

firmBrand() is built but on no route, which is why nothing binds. Its query is SELECT id, name always and only adds the logo and colour columns when configured — so the firm's real name works today; only the image and the colour wait on the master team.

Recommended: one Restify route; no screen, because the values live on the firm profile page in Advisor-e (his 2026-08-15 ruling). Ship on the name alone — the words “Firm logo” leave the plan on day one and the logo and colour arrive later with nothing more to build.

One backend route, no new screen, shipped without waiting for Advisor-e?
