Three things I have changed, and I need your word on each

  • The cyan border is replaced, not removed — RULED BY MIKE 2026-09-22. It was Advisor-e's frame, so taking the cyan off was right; leaving the sheet plain was not. The border is back on all 32 drawings, in the advisor firm's colour, which Advisor-e already holds on the firm profile page and already brands its own borders from. Click the three firms above and the edge changes with the mark. What settled it: the firm's colour drove exactly one element on this page — the disc — so once the disc became a fallback, a firm with a real logo would have had its colour appear nowhere at all.
  • The page number is gone. It was your deck's numbering; in the client's plan the pages are in the advisor's own running order, so your number would be wrong on the page.
  • The firm's mark is the firm's real logo — RULED BY MIKE 2026-09-22. The monogram disc was our stand-in because a real logo is an image of unknown shape, and that is no longer a reason: the logo sits in a fixed-height box with preserveAspectRatio="xMinYMid meet", so any proportion is scaled to fit and never stretched or cropped. Hartley's mark is a wide wordmark and Northbridge's a square emblem, deliberately — one box, visibly holding both. Kestrel Partners has no logo, which is how the disc now earns its place: it is the fallback, not the design.
