What this needs from you — four questions

  2. The two words on the toggle. Drawn as Summary / Every line. Ours, and never put to you. Alternatives: Headlines / Detail, or Short / Full.
  3. Should an overhead line with no figure in any month be hidden, as drawn? Recommended: yes, hide it. Fifteen rows of zeroes is how a useful screen becomes one people stop reading. The risk runs the other way, and it is real: a junior never sees that an “Insurance” line exists and never fills it in.
  4. Does the cash tab get the same treatment? It is not drawn here. Recommended: yes, and in the same change rather than later — it already itemises seven payment rows, so leaving it half-detailed makes it the odd one out. Drawn separately only because this page is long enough to read.
