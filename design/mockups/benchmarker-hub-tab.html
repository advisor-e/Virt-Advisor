Industry Benchmarks

The release in force is what every Business Performance Report compares a client against. Every figure on this screen and on the report's benchmark page is Stats NZ's own. Nothing here is typed, and nothing is invented: a file with a column missing is refused by the column's name, and a ratio Stats NZ did not publish prints as unpublished, never as a zero.

Replace the release

Stats NZ publishes the benchmarker as two CSV files a year, from stats.govt.nz › Business performance benchmarker. Both are needed, and nothing is stored until both read cleanly. Each file is read by its column names, so a file from a different year works so long as Stats NZ has not renamed a column — and if they have, the refusal names the column.

File What it carries Choose
Benchmark ratios required benchmark_ratios_all_industries-<year>-anzsic-class.csv — the eight ratios by industry and size band: 25th percentile, median, 75th percentile, and each band's turnover range
Financial required financial_all_industries-<year>.csv — business and employee counts, income, expenditure, profit and assets, with Stats NZ's accuracy category and suppression marks

What happens on Replace. Both files are read; the screen shows what it found — the year, how many industries, how many with benchmarks, how many published ratios — and the release goes into force for every report from then on. The previous release stays in the version history below, and Restore brings it back in one click.
