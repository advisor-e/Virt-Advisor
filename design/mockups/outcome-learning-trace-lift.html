Three questions — one recommendation each, one yes/no each

1 · The four "outweighed by" phrasings, as drawn?

The built line ends "…outweighed by your firm’s distinction". Three new endings join it, one per evidence kind: "by the main issue you confirmed", "by the client’s industry", "by what I heard in your description". Each names the thing the advisor did or said in the advisor's own terms, in the panel's first-person voice ("what you told me today").

Recommended: yes, as drawn. The trace already names every reason in the advisor's language, and "which evidence outweighed it" is the one fact story 3 says the trace must carry. Against: four phrasings are four strings in eight languages, and "what I heard in your description" is the vaguest of the four — but it is honest, because a signal is heard from the cause answer rather than picked from a list, and naming the signal's internal label would mean nothing to an advisor.

2 · The lifted line, as drawn: "+2 Break-Even — lifted in {where} · 6 firms · 31 cases"?

The built hold-back line with one word changed: "held back" becomes "lifted", the badge turns green, the sign turns plus. Same counts, same place, same note beneath.

Recommended: yes, as drawn. A lift is the same thing as a hold-back pointing the other way — one balance, one cap, one mentor decision — so the line should be the same line with the direction changed; an advisor who has learned to read one reads the other unasked. Against: "lifted" alone does not say why (it landed well); but neither does "held back" today, and the mentor's page is where the why lives.

3 · Does "Answered by" show on every trace, or only when the backup answered?

Screen B1 is the everyday case: one word, "OpenAI". Screen B2 is the rare case: the backup's name and the reason.

Recommended: always show it — one word on the everyday trace. A row that only appears when something went wrong cannot be trusted by its absence: an advisor cannot tell "the usual service answered" from "the row was not there". Seen every day, its change on the day the backup answers is unmissable, and a saved case read months later still says which service wrote it. Against: one more row on a panel that is already long, carrying the same word almost every time.

What this drawing does not decide The mentor's page listing lifts beside hold-backs is drawing 2's revision. The reason codes' internal names (pooled:lifted-<n>, pooled:outweighed-<kind>), the resolver's ordering, the provider seam and the trace block's shape are the plan's, pinned by tests, not by this screen.
