Decision 11 · how spoken words reach the right field — RULED

Ruled AS RECOMMENDED — Mike, 2026-09-16. The open field claims the words spoken while it is open, from the session's navigation timeline. The AI tidies wording and NEVER decides placement. 🔴 A build that hands the transcript and the field list to a model and asks it to sort them is building what this ruling refused — and it breaks the standing rule that LLM output is never trusted as structured data. Mike's question, 2026-09-16: "i want the words recorded into set fields - not a general summary - else how will it know to aportion text to specific questions?" He is right: a transcript summarised as one block is useless to a screen made of Strengths, Suppliers and Substitutes.

The Planner already knows what was on screen and when. The recording already knows the clock. Joining those two apportions every passage without a single judgement call: SWOT → Strengths was open from 14:02 to 14:09, so that passage is Strengths.

The AI's only job is then to turn that passage of speech into the sentence an advisor would have typed. That keeps it inside the standing rule that LLM output is never trusted as structured data — the structure comes from the clock, not the model — and it produces the Original / AI Suggestion / Final Approved Value trail the Engineering Standards require, because the raw passage stays beside the tidied one and the advisor keeps or rejects it.

The honest cost, and it is real. This only works if the advisor drives the Planner as the conversation moves. Run the session on paper with a recorder going and you get a transcript and nothing apportioned. Two things soften it: a passage can be dragged into a different field afterwards, and the whole transcript stays readable, so nothing is ever lost — only unsorted. It also means the store must keep a small navigation timeline: which field was open, from when, to when.
