Executive summary

A strong year with one watch-point: cash tied up in stock
Sales grew in all four quarters, led by repeat customers (+19%).
Gross margin held at 41% despite supplier price increases.
Stock on hand grew faster than sales — $118K extra cash tied up.
Customers are paying 5 days slower than last year (38 → 43 days).

Insight and watch-point

Every $1 of sales returned 13.9¢ of profit — up from 13.2¢ last year. Wage costs are the fastest-growing expense (+9%).
The December dip (–$57K) coincides with peak stock purchases. Consider staggering orders or a seasonal overdraft facility.

The three next steps

Free up cash from stock
Run down slow-moving lines ($46K) and trim reorder points on category C. Target: back to 60 days on hand — releasing roughly $45K in cash.
Tighten up debtor collection
Move key accounts to 14-day terms and automate reminders. Every 5 days faster releases about $39K.
Protect the margin
Review the top-10 supplier agreements before the next price round; wages are growing faster than sales.
