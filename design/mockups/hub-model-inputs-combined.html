Tax & Forecast Rates

Depreciation Rates

These are the rates the Three-Way Forecast writes assets down at, for clients in one country. Nothing here is any country's tax rules until a document has been loaded and approved — until then the forecast uses the app's own six figures, which are a starting point and are marked as one.

Tax Rates

These are the tax figures the Three-Way Forecast applies to clients in one country — the rate their profit is taxed at, the GST or VAT rate, how often they file a return, and whether GST follows the invoice or the payment. What does not change

  • Who sees what is unchanged. Forecast Trend Thresholds stays the mentor's; Property Tax Rules stays off the mentor's page; Depreciation Rates and Tax Rates are on every tier's.
  • Tax Rates keeps its own heading. Your 9 September rename was so a name predicts what is beneath it; Tax Rates is its own section under its own name, never folded into Depreciation Rates.
  • Country Rate Schedules stays its own tab. It is the global group manager's library of whole country schedules, not in your list.
