The blue box is the ruling, drawn. The AI receives the eight measures and their bands, and where step 1 named an industry, whether each sits below, within or above the Stats NZ middle half. Never the client's name, a dollar figure, a file, or the industry's own name. The box lists what is sent rather than describing it, because a description is the app deciding what to disclose; it is built from the same figures the pages use, so it cannot disagree with the report.

Why the industry's name is not sent. The bands alone identify nobody. A named industry plus a size band plus eight positions starts to narrow the field, and the draft does not need it: "stock days are red and below the industry" is what the AI acts on, and the advisor adds the context in the edit, which is where their judgement belongs. If you want the industry name sent, that is one line in the box and a separate yes.

The button is disabled until the score exists — with one year of accounts there are no bands, and the box says so instead of sending an empty list.

The tick IS the approval gate the standards require. Financial output needs an explicit isApproved: true before AI output is committed, and an advisor reading three next steps and deciding they are fit for the client is that approval. There is no second button labelled Approve.

It applies to typed words too. Today page 8 prints whatever is in the three boxes. With a draft in play the tick has to cover the final words whichever way they arrived, so the gate is on the words, not on the draft: an advisor who types all three still ticks. That is one rule instead of two, and it is why the badge says edited per field — the record has to show what the AI proposed, what the advisor changed, and what was approved, the Original / AI Suggestion / Final Approved discipline the standards already require.

Every draft is counted, and the tick names which one went in — the rule from the economic analysis, unchanged. Draft again stays because the commonest reason to redraft is that the accounts were corrected on step 2, not a disliked answer; the count makes a re-roll visible rather than impossible. Drafting again clears the tick and overwrites only the fields the advisor has not edited.

What the draft is not allowed to do. It cannot invent a dollar figure, because it never receives one — the sample above has the advisor adding "$45K" in the edit, not the AI. Any figure in an untouched draft is refused on the server before it reaches the screen, on the same rule the economic analysis uses. It cannot name a template or a service the firm sells; it is three plain priorities.
