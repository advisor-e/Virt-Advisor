What this says

This property costs the client $929 a week for the first seven years. It turns cash-positive in year 8, once the principal-and-interest loan is repaid. Over ten years it builds $846,798 of equity against $620,859 of money put in — a return of 36.4%, which only turns positive in year 9.

No tax is payable until year 10: the rental losses are ring-fenced and carried forward, and the interest deduction phases out over the first five years.
Deliberately absent from Phase 1: the family home and its mortgage, the loan apportionment across five properties, properties 2–5, and the consolidated report. Wording still open (see §8): the screen's name, the four band labels, and whether the New Zealand tax rules are fixed or firm-editable.
