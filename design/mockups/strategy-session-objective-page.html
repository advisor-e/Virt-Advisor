The Session Objective Page

Page 2 of six of your decks is missing from the plan the app produces. It is the page you talk to at the start of a meeting — welcome, roles, housekeeping, what today is for. This drawing shows what the page actually is, how much of it is the same in every deck, and asks four questions before anything is built.

Drawn 2026-09-20. Every line of your page below was read out of the PDF's own text layer by machine, with your own sizes, weights and colours — not one word was retyped.

⚠ A correction to what I told you in chat, before anything else

I said the objective page “isn't lost — it prints near the end, on page 34”. That was wrong, and it made the problem sound smaller than it is.

Page 34 carries the Strategic Objective — the business outcome the client decides during the session. Your page 2 is a completely different thing: it is the meeting's ground rules, spoken before any work starts. None of its nine lines exists anywhere in the app — not in the data, not in the wording files, not in any screen. The page is missing outright, not relocated.

Your page, as it actually is

Pivot.pdf page 2 · 960×540pt · title 32pt, body 16pt · your two emphasised lines are bold and navy, the rest grey — all read from the file, none of it chosen by us.

Our Session Objective

Welcome, it’s all about you and your business today…

  • Everyone contributes to collaborative thinking.
  • Planned Breaks & Housekeeping
  • My/Our Role in the Planning Session is to Challenge What You Believe To Be True!
  • Your Role is to be Ready To Explore Issues/ Ideas With an Open Heart and an Open Mind.
  • The Objective (expectations) for Today’s meeting is that we ‘Discover What We’ve Been Missing’!
  • Record what we learn.
  • Build an action plan going forward.

The two navy lines are the two you chose to emphasise — both about roles. That is the page doing its real job: it sets the contract for the room.

What that tells us, and it decides the whole design

  • Eight of the nine lines are standing wording. Welcome, contributions, housekeeping, the two role lines, record what we learn, build an action plan. Four decks carry them identically; they are not written fresh for a session.
  • One line is genuinely about the day — “The Objective (expectations) for Today's meeting…”. Organisational Review proves it: there, the objective is an open choice between Internal Efficiencies, Team Engagement or both, with an instruction to spend 10–15 minutes deciding.
  • You personalise it when it matters. Bizz Targets' version names the client inside the line — “…collaborative thinking… (The Christchurch Engineers)”. ⚠ That is a real client's name sitting in a source deck. It must never travel into platform wording, and no drawing of ours reproduces it.
  • The agenda half is already solved. Where a deck's page 2 also carries an agenda, that agenda is exactly what the step builder now produces. This page is only the preamble.

The decisions

Four questions, each one recommendation and one answer a yes or no settles. They will be asked one at a time.

Decision 1 — does the page belong in the plan at all?

It is a page the advisor says as much as one the client reads. The app could simply not have it, and leave the advisor to open the meeting in their own words.

Recommendation: yes, it belongs — as page 2, before the agenda. Six of your decks carry it, your approved drawing lists it, and a client who is handed the plan afterwards gets the contract for the room alongside the work, exactly as your own deck does it.

Does the Session Objective page become page 2 of the plan?

Decision 2 — which of it is fixed and which is typed each time

Eight lines are the same in four decks. One is about today.

Recommendation: the eight are standing wording; the one objective line is typed per session, in a single box on the scope screen, and left blank prints the page without it. No client name is ever inserted automatically — your Bizz Targets version puts one inside the sentence, and choosing where a client's name sits in your copy is writing your copy.

Are the eight lines standing, with only the objective line typed per session?

Decision 3 — who can change the standing eight

Your words today. Another firm may open a room differently, and the platform rule is that anything shaping what a client sees is visible and editable on a screen, starting at the mentor tier.

Recommendation: build it on the Mentor Hub and there alone for now. The wording is platform content and is yours; a firm that genuinely opens its meetings differently is the moment to cascade it down, not before. Stating that judgement rather than assuming it is the rule.

Is the wording editable on the Mentor Hub only, for now?

Decision 4 — does the advisor see it on screen during the session

In the room this page is spoken, not read silently. It could appear as the first thing on the session screen, or only in the assembled document.

Recommendation: document only, and no new screen. The plan is what goes on the projector, so the page is already in front of both of them when it matters. A second place showing the same words is a second place they can disagree.

Does the page live in the document only, with no separate screen?
