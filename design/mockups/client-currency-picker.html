Three things I have assumed — say if any is wrong

  1. It sits on the report header, not the Model Library. The Model Library is firm-wide and has no client on it, so a per-client control could not work there. The header already knows the client.
  2. Any advisor may change it, not only a manager. That follows your ruling directly — the manager sets the firm’s, the advisor sets the client’s.
  3. It appears on all eleven client-aware reports at once. They share one header, so this is one change rather than eleven. The other thirty-seven report screens are calculators with no client and keep the firm’s currency.

The relabel line repeats here deliberately. A client-level currency reads as a conversion far more readily than a firm-wide one, which is why item 13.1 shipped that sentence first.
