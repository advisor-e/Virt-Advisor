Recording a strategy session in sections

Each concept card in Run session becomes its own short recording. The advisor presses the card's record button, and pressing the next card's button closes the one before. Each section is turned into text as soon as it closes and its audio is deleted straight after. The words then land in the box that was open when they were spoken. The advisor keeps, edits or rejects each one. second build — screen 4, not in the first build At the end the sections join into one transcript, so Meeting Review's summary and coaching notes work as they do now.

This joins two things you've already asked for: Decisions 10 and 11 of the planner drawing (16 Sep), and your option on 8.4 (24 Sep), "clicking a section starts its own recording … saved per section and combined at the end — which also shows a technical dropout sooner than the end of the meeting."

The impact test, stated before the design

  • The problem. A strategy session can't be recorded today. OpenAI refuses a file over 25 MB, which is about 27 minutes of browser audio (measured 24 Sep). Meeting Review sends the whole meeting as one file, so a planning session would be refused and its audio destroyed anyway. Every box in the planner is typed by hand, often while the client is still talking.
  • How we'll know.
    • A test session of at least 45 minutes is transcribed with no refusal. Today that's impossible.
    • Advisor and client are labelled correctly in every section, not only the first. Checked by reading every section of that test session.
    • Every passage lands in the box that was open when it was spoken. Checked against the navigation timeline on the same session.
    • How many suggested sentences the advisor keeps, edits or rejects. This is counted, not guessed. A low keep-rate says the tidy step isn't earning its place.
  • What already does this job, and why it isn't enough.
    • On-device dictation (12.2) gets the advisor's own words into a box, but only what the advisor chooses to say into the microphone. It never hears the client and never labels speakers.
    • Meeting Review's recorder hears everyone, but as one file. That fails past 27 minutes, and it only shows a failure at the very end.

Checked against the OpenAI contract rules (OPENAI-ZDR-CONSTRAINTS.md) and the privacy exception

  • Z1: audio goes to /v1/audio/transcriptions, which keeps nothing. The tidy step uses /v1/chat/completions. Both are allowed.
  • Z3: every passage sent for tidying passes the moderation check first, as moderate: spoken text. Meeting Review's reports already do this.
  • Z4, Z5, Z7: it's bound to one session, behind the login, and audio isn't a file input.
  • Z9: until OpenAI switches ZDR on for the project, nothing here may be described as covered by ZDR.
  • The privacy exception in CLAUDE.md: this is Meeting Review (Decision 10), so it sits inside the one exception and doesn't open a second. Internal IDs and firm or advisor identifiers are still stripped.
  • The firm declaration gate (Compliance P1) applies unchanged. If the firm hasn't declared, the record buttons are locked, exactly as /meeting-record is today.

How it works underneath

Record a sectionThe browser records the card's section in small pieces, which are saved as they arrive, as now. A crash costs seconds.
Close itThe next card's button, "End recording", or 25 minutes closes the section.
Turn it into textThat section alone goes to OpenAI with the advisor's 8-second voice sample, so the advisor is named correctly in every section.
Delete the audioAt once, whether or not that worked, and the deletion is checked, as now.
Match by the clockEach passage's time is laid against the navigation timeline. The box open at that moment claims it. No AI involved.
Tidy the wordingEach passage is moderation-checked, then tidied. It's offered under its box and kept only by the advisor.
Join at the endAll sections become one transcript for Meeting Review's two reports.

🔴 Why the 8-second voice sample is needed: the one hard problem

Today the code decides who the advisor is by who speaks first (attributeSpeakers). That's safe only because the consent line opens the recording. Section 2 onwards has no consent line. If the client speaks first, every label in that section is swapped, and nothing on screen looks wrong. OpenAI's documented fix is to send a 2–10 second sample of a known speaker (known_speaker_references) with each file. The consent line runs about 25 seconds, too long, so the browser keeps its first 8 seconds as a separate clip while it records. No audio is cut on the server, which matters because the locked Node 14.15 runs no audio tools.
