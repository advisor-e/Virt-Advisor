The client's reports, brought into "Assess current position"

Your Strategic Orientation page 2 asks the advisor to "Assess current position by reviewing (pre-meeting) data (section 2) & Financial Performance Reports". In the app, that step's form holds customer rows only, so the financial half has nowhere to go, even when the app already holds the client's Business Performance Report and three-way forecast. This drawing adds one button to that card. It brings both reports' headline figures in, run on the reports' own maths, and the client's plan prints them. Nothing is copied and nothing links out: the same route your Business Owner Expectations card took (15.23).

The impact test, stated before the design

  • The gain. The advisor assesses the current position from the client's real figures, in the session, and the client's plan carries them. Today they retype figures the app already holds, or skip the step.
  • How we'll know.
    • Headline figures reaching this step and the plan PDF without typing: 0 today.
    • Every figure equals what the report shows on its own screen for that client. One test per figure proves it.
    • Walked in a browser, before and after.
  • What already does this job. Opening each report in another tab. It works in the room, but nothing lands in the plan, and it's the patchwork you ruled out on 16 Sep.

A

The build measures this page on an A4 landscape sheet before it ships, as every other plan page is measured.

What this drawing does NOT bring in

  • Staff, customer and supplier feedback, and the economic outlook. Your page names these as part of the same assessment. No report in the app holds any of them. They stay with the advisor and the Customer & Skills Review form.
  • The other twelve saved reports. They can come in the same way later. This drawing starts with the two your request named, the performance reports and the three-way forecast.
  • Six reports that save nothing. High-Level Budget, Mid-Level Budget, Stock Purchasing, Sales Dashboard, Wages Review and Retirement Review have no saved figures to bring in.

The decisions this drawing needs

Asked one at a time, each as a yes or no. Each gets written back here once you've ruled.

Decision A · a button, rather than figures that appear on their own

Recommended: a button, "Bring in the reports", as your request described.

The advisor chooses the moment, for example after a report has been finished before the session. Business Owner Expectations loads by itself because the card is the model. Here the reports are someone else's work being brought in, and your words asked for a button.

Decision B · which figures come in

Recommended: each report's own leading figures, under its own labels. For the Business Performance Report that's net profit, the health score and its eight coloured measures. For the forecast, the result, cash at the end, the lowest point and gross margin.

These are what each report puts first on its own screen, so they're the figures you already chose to lead with. The full detail stays in the report itself.

Decision C · a report not saved for this client

Recommended: say so in one line, with no link out to the report.

A link would leave the session, which is the patchwork you ruled out on 16 Sep.

Decision D · what reaches the client's printed plan

Recommended: one "Where the business stands" page, printed only when the reports were brought in.

Your page makes the reports part of assessing the position, so the plan that follows should show the position it was built on.

The wording

Every label on the report tiles is the report's own. Everything else is proposed and needs your ruling: "This client's financial reports", "Bring in the reports", "Bring in again", the two lines under them, "up from" under net profit, the "saved … by" line, the "no report saved" sentence, and the page title "Where the business stands".
