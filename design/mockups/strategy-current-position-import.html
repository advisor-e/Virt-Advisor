The client's Business Performance Report, brought into "Assess current position"

Your Strategic Orientation page 2 asks the advisor to "Assess current position by reviewing (pre-meeting) data (section 2) & Financial Performance Reports". In the app, that step's form holds customer rows only, so the financial half has nowhere to go, even when the app already holds the client's Business Performance Report. This drawing adds one button to that card. It brings in the whole report, every page and chart, worked out on the report's own maths and drawn exactly as the report draws it. Nothing is copied and nothing links out: the same route your Business Owner Expectations card took (15.23).

The impact test, stated before the design

  • The gain. The advisor assesses the current position from the client's real results, in the session, and the client's plan carries them. Today they retype figures the app already holds, or skip the step.
  • How we'll know.
    • Headline figures reaching this step and the plan PDF without typing: 0 today.
    • Every figure equals what the report shows on its own page for that client. A test proves the card asks the report's route exactly what the report's page asks.
    • Walked in a browser, before and after.
  • What already does this job. Opening the report in another tab. It works in the room, but nothing lands in the plan, and it's the patchwork you ruled out on 16 Sep.
