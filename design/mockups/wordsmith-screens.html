Wordsmith: the planner panel and the hub tab

Two places. In Run session, the advisor presses Wordsmith on the Alignment Statements card once its recorded section has been turned into text, says what the statements are for and how they should sound, and gets a draft of each of the five statements to edit and approve. In the hub, each manager shapes what Wordsmith knows: the definitions, the writing rules, the questions, the word limits and the style wording, cascading down on the standard rules. What each screen shows, and who may change it, was ruled on wordsmith-style-settings.html; this page draws it.

Already settled, and not asked again here

  • Only the Alignment Statements recording is ever sent, with the session's confirmed consent (your privacy ruling, 2026-09-28). Checked against the twelve OpenAI rules at scoping; it passes.
  • The five steps and what each reads, the five domain writing rules, and who can shape what, all approved 2026-09-29.
  • How Wordsmith is measured is open, on your ruling. Whatever measure you choose, the build records each statement's AI draft, the final approved wording and the time taken, so it can be counted later.

Got it

This is what Wordsmith knows when it writes a client's Vision, Purpose, Values, Mission and Strategy from what they said in the Alignment Statements section. What you change here reaches the levels below you.

  1. Pick a statement, or Style wording.
  2. Edit, switch off or add a row. It saves when you press Save.

The decisions this drawing needs

Each goes to you on its own, in this order, with the recommendation beside it.

Decision A · when the Wordsmith button can be pressed RULED 2026-09-29: yes, as recommended

Recommended: only once the Alignment Statements card's recorded section has been turned into text; greyed before, with the reason.

Wordsmith writes only from what was said, so before the text exists there is nothing to write from.

Decision B · changing the settings Wordsmith read from the style RULED 2026-09-29: yes, as recommended

Recommended: the advisor can change any of the four settings and press "Write again with these".

The AI's reading of "professional" may not be the room's. Changing a setting costs one more writing pass.

Decision C · answering a question for the room RULED 2026-09-29: yes, as recommended

Recommended: the advisor types the room's answer under the question and presses "Write again with this answer"; the answer counts as something the client said.

A Vision with no date and a Mission with no measure are incomplete by your own definitions. Without this, the only way to add the date is to edit it in by hand.

Decision D · who agrees to a statement before it goes in the box RULED 2026-09-29: yes, as recommended

Recommended: a tick in the concept summaries' shape, "The client has read this statement and agrees with it" (wording corrected on Mike's yes, 2026-09-29: the summaries' own is "…read this summary and agrees…"), before "Use this wording" can be pressed.

The wording goes into the client's plan and possibly a bank submission. The tick records that they agreed, as the summaries already do.

Decision E · the communication table under the five statements RULED 2026-09-29: yes, as recommended

Recommended: Wordsmith writes the five statements only; the Who We're Talking To / What We Say / What We Mean table stays as the advisor types it, for now.

The engine was built and measured for the five statements. The table could come later, once the five are in use.
