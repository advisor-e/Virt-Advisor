What is yours, what is ours, and one more bug in my own tooling

  • Three of these charts are your pictures, unchanged. Market Diffusion, Product Life Cycle and Deming are images in your deck, not vector drawings — there is nothing to copy point for point, and tracing a curve by eye is the one thing the method forbids. They are lifted straight out of the PDF, which leaves them free of the Advisor-e logo, and placed at exactly the rectangle your page places them at. On your word, 2026-09-18. They are rasters, so they will not sharpen the way the drawn ones do when scaled, and they cannot take a firm's colours.
  • 🔴 The Sigmoid is drawn — and my reader nearly hid it. It was discarding any path whose fill read as black, and a stroke-only curve reports exactly that. Four pages came back as "no vector content" when they had plenty. Had it stood, I would have traced these curves by hand — the Porter's failure, repeated. The reader now asks the path whether it is stroked, filled or both, instead of guessing from a colour.
  • Your words are no longer typed by anyone. Every <text> on these four sheets was generated straight from the PDF's text layer — position, size, weight, italic, colour and the width each line occupies. Your ligatures become their letters and nothing else changes.
