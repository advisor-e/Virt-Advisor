The staff register — the gate, in its three states

The register holds a client's named employees with their pay, accrued leave, length of service and what the business loses if they leave. This strip is what decides whether any of that is on screen at all.

The one decision — the wording of this middle state

Recommended, and drawn above: the pill reads Available, the heading "A due-diligence project is open on this client's case.", and the button "Open the staff register".

Why the sentence says what the register holds before it is opened. The advisor is about to create a document listing named colleagues of their client with a rating attached. Saying so at the moment of the decision is what makes the switch a decision rather than a click — and Decision 6's whole reasoning is that the switch is "what makes it a dated, attributable act, which is exactly what makes it defensible."

Why "Available" rather than a second "Closed". Closed and Available are different answers to the advisor: one says the app will not do this, the other says the app is waiting for you to decide. Drawing both as "Closed" would put a button on a strip that reads closed.

RULED BY MIKE 2026-09-15 — the switch turns both ways

Decision 6 named only the switch-on and the automatic close when the case leaves the due-diligence domain. An advisor who opened the register on the wrong client had no way back — and that client is necessarily ANOTHER client in due diligence, since the gate refuses a switch-on for any other, so the automatic close would never fire for them. Ruled: a "Close the staff register" switch, on the open state.

Nothing is erased. A close is recorded exactly as an opening is — who and when — and it KEEPS the opening it closed, so the record reads as a decision taken and a decision reversed. That is a fuller history than the one-way switch, not a thinner one, and firmOverlay's version history holds every earlier switch besides.

A closed register returns to Available, not to a fourth state. Once closed, the truth is exactly what Available already says: a due-diligence project stands and the register is not open. A fourth sentence would describe a situation the third already describes. Re-opening is a fresh decision and is recorded as one.

Closing needs no due-diligence case, unlike opening. It is the safe direction: refusing to close because the case has moved on would leave an advisor unable to shut a register the app already treats as closed.
