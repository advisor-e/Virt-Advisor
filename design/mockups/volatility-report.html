COACH What to say about this

Two-thirds of the months land inside the normal range, which is what the model predicts. Going up and down is normal. An owner who reacts to every month above or below the average is permanently passing or failing against a line that was never a target.

Four months sit outside the range and none is beyond the second deviation. The report shows where a month sits. Whether it is seasonal, a one-off, or something the business did to itself is a judgement for you and the client — not for the arithmetic.
