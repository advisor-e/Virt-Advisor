Coaching Reference — proposed Firm Manager tab

Nothing here is built. The engine half of item 4.9 shipped on 2026-08-15: the fifteen coaching rows now cascade down every tier, so a firm's decisions about them would be honoured. There is no screen, so no firm can make a decision for the engine to honour. This is that screen, for approval.

The rows below are the real content of data/coaching-reference.json, not sample text — fifteen rows of five prose fields each. That volume is why the rows are closed by default and open where you click, rather than laid out flat the way the Advisory Staircase's short steps are. The single "Your firm" entry is the one invention on the page, and it is labelled as such — no firm has ever added one, because there is no screen to do it with.

Two things need your word: the wording (every new sentence is listed at the foot of this page) and whether this shape is right.

What is deliberately not on this screen

Three things a sibling tab has and this one does not

No "Platform updated this entry" badge, and no Adopt / Keep mine panel. The Advisory Staircase can spot when Advisor-e changes a step a firm has already edited, because it stores a fingerprint of what the firm was shown at the time. The coaching engine that shipped stores no such fingerprint, so this screen cannot honestly tell a firm that anything changed. Building the badge without it would be a light that never comes on. It is a separate piece of work if you want it.

No version history. Same reason — the staircase's history list belongs to its single whole-config setting, and the coaching rows have no equivalent.

No template picker on your own entries. An entry you add still has to name a template that exists, or the AI is coached toward something it cannot find. Whether that is a free-text box or a list to pick from is a question for you, and it is the one gap in this design I would want settled before building.
