The Education Gate

When the engine can see a client is not comfortable reading their own numbers, the advisor is asked — before any recommendation — whether to lead with education or go straight to what is technically needed. Two screens: what the advisor sees, and where the mentor changes it.

1. What the advisor sees

It appears after the last domain question and before any recommendation. It asks once per case.

Advisor

Client keeps chasing turnover and won't look at margin. They've taken on two more staff on the back of a record sales month.

Advisor-e

From what you've described, this client may not yet be comfortable reading their own numbers.

Do you want me to put education first, or show what's technically needed and leave the teaching to you?

Education first. I’ll lead with the material that builds the client’s understanding, then the technical work.
Why you were asked. This came up because you mentioned “chasing turnover”.
The reason line is not optional. Mike’s 2026-07-16 ruling says the reasoning is shown either way — so it appears after both answers, not just this one. If the engine cannot name the phrase that triggered it, the sentence is dropped, never replaced with something vague. A gate that cannot say why it fired should say nothing.
