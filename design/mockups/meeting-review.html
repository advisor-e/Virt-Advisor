The pre-set is what makes the coaching notes trustworthy. Asking a model "how did this advisor do?" produces fluent invention. Asking it "find where they framed the meeting, quote it, or answer NOT FOUND" is a search with a citation — something models are dependable at.

It also pays before a word is recorded: the advisor walks in holding the list, with the firm's script one tap away.

✅ Approved 2026-09-01 · read aloud, by you, first Before we begin — I'd like to record our meeting. The recording is turned into a written transcript by AI, and the recording itself is deleted as soon as that's done. It gives you a written summary of what we agree today, and it helps me review how I did. Nothing is shared outside our firm. Is everyone here happy for me to record?

The audio is transcribed and then deleted. The transcript is kept for 18 months and is visible to you and your firm — never to Advisor-e.

18 months — your ruling of 2026-09-01. Long enough to clear an annual meeting cycle with six months to spare, so year-on-year follow-through works; short enough that a client hearing it does not pause.

⚠ That number must not be typed into the sentence when this is built. A firm can move its own retention dial, and this line quotes the figure back to a client out loud. 18 months is the platform default, not a constant — hardcode it and advisors are saying something untrue the day a firm changes the setting, with nothing on screen to show it.

This is the correction you caught, drawn as a screen. The order is record, then speak, then confirm. An earlier draft ticked "I have read the consent line aloud" before recording began — past tense, which puts the client's agreement outside the audio and defeats the point of having it.
  • Repeating the line is deliberate — it stops an advisor paraphrasing from memory, and paraphrasing the AI clause out of existence.
  • It also anchors who is who. You speak first, so the software knows which voice is the advisor's. No stored voice sample, so no biometric data.

Three things these screens carry.
  • "Stop and delete" never leaves the screen. It answers a client who says "actually, can you turn that off?" — and it destroys the audio and any transcript already made from it.
  • Audio leaves in short pieces as it is captured, so a crash costs seconds rather than the meeting. There is no second take with a real client.
  • The alarm is the honest half of choosing live capture. Uploading a file was the safer recommendation and you chose live, which is right for consent and speed — but an operating system can suspend a background tab. So it fails loudly: a tidy page of "no observations" must never be what total failure looks like.

Four rules are visible here, and each is load-bearing.
  • Every finding quotes the transcript or declares the thing absent. An observation with no citation is thrown away rather than shown — "NOT FOUND" must be as easy for the model to answer as a quote, or it invents evidence to be helpful.
  • You can disagree with any of it, and the disagreement stays. That is the line between coaching and surveillance, and the only honest source of data for improving the observation points.
  • The drawing check is your ruling of 2026-09-01 — your words raise it, the card says out loud that it is guessing, and you settle it in one tap. What gets stored is your answer, never the guess, or a maybe hardens into a fact on its way to the manager's figures.
  • It is yours. No manager at any tier reads this unless you send it.

Are the observation points landing?

Not enough meetings yet to show patterns safely. Figures appear once at least 5 advisors and 20 meetings have contributed. Below that, a count can be worked backwards to an individual — and no manager sees an individual's notes unless that advisor sends them.
This is the screen most firms will actually meet, which is why it is drawn. Your ruling of 2026-09-01: 5 advisors and 20 meetings before any figure appears.
  • The cost is real and you accepted it. A four-advisor firm sees no manager figures, ever. They still set the points, and their advisors still get their own notes.
  • The empty state must explain itself, or the first manager to open it reports a bug — when the honest answer is that the software is protecting their people.
  • It is not a tuning constant. Lowering it to populate a screen would undo the promise quietly, and no test would catch it.
