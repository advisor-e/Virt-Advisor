The Banded Grid

One capture form, five of your tables. Every heading, worked example and line number below is copied from your own fill-in documents — nothing on this page was written by us. This is a drawing of how the form would look on screen, for approval before it is built.

What the screen does today — measured in the running app, 2026-09-19

  • Blue Ocean Fronts loses one of your headings and one of your boxes. On screen the first block is headed “1, Enter your thoughts here…” — your placeholder line, promoted to a heading — and your real heading, What Are Our ‘Red Water’ Competition Fronts?, is nowhere on the page. It offers 14 boxes where your document gives 15.
  • The 8 Profit Levers offers 29 boxes where your document has 28, and the extra one is your own column heading “Task” turned into a box to type in.
  • Both faults come from the same reading mistake, and both are fixed by the rule below.

⚠ A correction to the first version of this page

Version 1 said “Porter's renders 32 text boxes with every band heading lost”. That was wrong. Porter's does not use this renderer at all — it has its own approved five-force card from 2026-09-16 and is untouched by any of this. The tables that DO use this renderer already show your headings; the two faults above are specific, and are what the screen was measured to do.

The rule this form follows

  • A row where every cell carries words is a band — its cells are the column headings for the lines beneath it.
  • A ruled line is a box to fill in. Where you number your lines, the number is shown beside the box rather than inside it.
  • A line that carries words is your worked example — shown, never typed into.

Voice notes — the same bar the advisor already uses

Nothing new is invented and no wording is ours. This is the voice bar from “I have a client with a problem…” — the same control, the same three states, the same words, on every box. An advisor who has dictated an answer to the Virtual Advisor already knows how to fill in a capture table.

What that means here

  • The words go straight into the box being spoken to. Nothing has to work out afterwards where a sentence belongs — the advisor picked the box by tapping it.
  • Nothing leaves the browser. No audio is uploaded, nothing is stored and no transcript is made, so none of Meeting Review's consent gates apply — exactly as it is today in the Virtual Advisor.
  • The advisor reads it before it is kept, and can edit it in the box or record again. Same as the chat.
  • Where a browser has no speech support the bar is simply absent and the box is typed into, which is what the rest of the app already does.

One thing this is NOT, said plainly

  • This is dictation into a box, not a recording of the whole session. Recording a meeting end to end is Meeting Review, and that is still behind the two non-coding gates in item 8.1. If you want the session recorded as well as dictated, that is a second thing and it is gated.
