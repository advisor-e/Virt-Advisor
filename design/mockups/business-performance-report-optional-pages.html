Business Performance Report — the four optional pages, drawn for approval

Item 4.70, 2026-09-08. These replace the three optional pages that were on the dropdown only because a model existed — The Eight Levers, Debtor Drag and Valuation — with pages that answer the questions an owner asks of a performance report and that the accounts can honestly support. Same landscape composition, same palette and density as the approved report; the style sheet is the approved drawing's, copied, not redrawn. Every figure is Harbourside's sample data and every footer says so.

The four pages and what each is for

  • Why profit changed — a bridge from last year's profit to this year's: sales growth, margin, overheads, and the rest. Both profit and loss exports; nothing typed.
  • Where the cash went — from this year's profit to the movement in the bank. Two balance sheets and one profit and loss. Drawings are the residual of the equity movement and the page says so.
  • What moves profit — what a one percent change in price, volume, cost of sales and overheads each does to profit, ranked; the break-even point and how far sales can fall before a loss. This year's profit and loss only.
  • Stock against the accounts — the Cin7 Core or Unleashed export read beside the balance sheet and the cash page: does the file agree with the accounts, where the stock value sits, how much is already sold, who is funding the shelf. Offered only once a stock export has been dropped in. Built after the stage 4 reader.

What the pages refuse to do

  • Sales growth is not split into price and volume — no set of accounts carries units. The bridge says "more sales at last year's margin".
  • What-moves-profit treats cost of sales as moving with sales and overheads as fixed, and prints that assumption beside the figures.
  • No ageing or slow-moving figure comes from the stock file — neither package's export carries a date. That stays the advisor's typed figure on page 6.

One thing for you to rule

With all four added beside the Outlook page the report reaches sixteen pages counting the cover and the Important Information page — one over the fifteen you set. The drawing counts them as pages 11 to 14 and leaves the cap as it is; the dropdown below simply stops offering pages once the cap is reached, so the advisor chooses. Say the word if the cap should count content pages only. Ruled 2026-09-08: the fifteen applies to the base report only; optional pages have no cap.

Added 2026-09-08 for stage 5 — the fifth page, Sales Volatility

  • Sales Volatility (page 15 below) — how steady the twelve months of sales were: the average month, the usual range one deviation either side of it, which months fell outside, and the score. It is the Volatility Report's own arithmetic on the by-month Profit and Loss dropped at step 2, so the two screens can never disagree; the band words are the Volatility Report's, already ruled.
  • Offered only once twelve complete months are on file. A by-month export taken mid-year gives fewer, and last year's export fills the rest — the same two-file rule as the Volatility Report.
  • Nothing on it is typed. The three sentences are built from the figures; the advisor's watch-point stays on page 7.
