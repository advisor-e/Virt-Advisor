The blue box is the ruling, drawn. "The advisor writes the research brief and sees the exact words that will be sent. The app sends nothing about the client on its own." It repeats the brief rather than summarising it, because a summary is the app deciding what to disclose. It updates as they type.

🔴 THIS SCREEN CANNOT BE BUILT AS DRAWN, and the reason is the drawing's own principle. The API reports the model's search calls, but it does not say which of the five output sections a search belongs to. Mapping a query onto "Global", "Ireland", "Galway", "The sector" would be a guess presented as progress — which is precisely what the note below forbids.

What slice 2 builds instead: the model's REAL search phrases, as they arrive — "Searching: Ireland CPI August 2026". More honest, more interesting to watch, and it still answers "which area is it stuck on". The backend already collects them (GET /api/report/economic-analysis/:runId returns searches).

Everything else on this screen stands: the timing, the "you can leave this page", and the polling shape.
The four lines are not decoration and not a fake progress bar. They are the four research areas the prompt asks for, ticked as the model's own search calls come back — 9 to 12 of them per run. If a real one is slow, the honest thing on screen is which area it is still working on.

"You can leave this page" is true and load-bearing: at roughly a hundred seconds this cannot be an inline wait, so it returns a job and the page polls — the pattern server/routes/meetingReview.js already uses twice for the same reason.

Economic analysis

3 · Sector outlook

Private health insurance provides a substantial, but not universal, source of access to private care. The Health Insurance Authority reported 2.55 million people insured at 31 December 2025, an annual increase of 1.2%. The average adult premium was €1,902 at 1 March 2026, following a 9% increase during 2025. hia.ie The HIA's Q1 2026 bulletin reported the same insured population of 2.55 million, after a quarterly increase of 2,936 people, and an average adult premium of €1,960. hia.ie The difference reflects a later reporting point and premium measure.

There is evidence of capacity pressure in primary-care therapies. The HSE reported in a parliamentary response that the longest recorded wait for a physiotherapy assessment in Galway was 256 weeks at August 2025. hse.ie This is a dated maximum waiting-time measure, not a current average wait, patient count, referral pipeline or measure of the clinic's addressable demand.

Public feedback was reviewed as a qualitative rather than quantitative source. A Galway discussion forum in July 2026 contained recommendations for multiple named physiotherapy providers across the city and county. reddit.com These posts are self-selected anecdotes. They cannot establish clinical quality, waiting times, market share, patient numbers or demand, but they do indicate that patients can identify several alternative providers and that local competition should not be assumed to be limited.

4 · What this means for the business under review

The planned third site would open during a period of positive domestic demand but persistent cost pressure. Inflation in Ireland is being influenced materially by energy, while service-price inflation remains elevated. This is relevant to utilities, cleaning, insurance, supplies, travel and household purchasing capacity.

The finance requirement has two separate economic exposures: a one-off fit-out and equipment purchase, and an ongoing lease commitment. International energy and freight disruption may affect supplier lead times and landed equipment costs. Domestic commercial borrowing conditions remain relatively expensive by euro-area comparison. Actual supplier quotations, lease terms and lender pricing are not known.

5 · What could not be sourced

Seven gaps, each saying what it would have been used for. This section is why a credit assessor trusts the rest.

  • A current Galway commercial rent series for clinical premises. National office and retail expectations were available; no authoritative series for this property type and locality.
  • The number of active private physiotherapy clinics, practitioners, waiting times or prices in Galway. Needed to assess local competitive intensity.
  • The clinic's insurer-panel status, reimbursement rates or insured-patient share. Needed to assess the stability of the private-insurance channel.

Screen 5 — the second tick, which is the approval
Include in the report the client receives
Adds the economic analysis to the printed forecast pack, after the cash flow. Your name, today's date and which run this is are recorded against it.
This tick IS the approval gate the standards require. Financial and regulatory work needs an explicit isApproved: true before AI output is committed, and an advisor reading the research and deciding it is fit for a lender is that approval. So there is no second button labelled Approve — there is this, and it records who ticked it and when.

It is separate from the research tick on Mike's ruling of 2026-09-06, so an advisor can run the research, read it, and decide not to send it. That is a real and useful outcome, not a failure.

What it cannot do is silently include unread research. The tick is disabled until the research has been generated, and re-running the research clears it.

🔴 EVERY RUN IS COUNTED, AND THE APPROVAL NAMES WHICH ONE WENT IN — ruled 2026-09-06. The question put to Mike was whether Research again should exist at all, since re-rolling until the answer flatters the client is the one habit this feature must not encourage. It stays, because the commonest reason to re-run is a BAD BRIEF, not a disliked answer — an advisor who forgets to say they import from Europe gets research with no freight in it, and a feature with no way to correct its own input is worse than one that can be misused.

So the risk is made visible instead of impossible. What is stored when the tick is set is "run 4 of 4, included by [advisor], 6 September 2026". One run looks like what it is; seven runs and the friendliest chosen is on the record and readable by the firm manager. This is the standards' own Original / AI Suggestion / Final Approved discipline applied to a re-run, not a new rule invented for this screen.
