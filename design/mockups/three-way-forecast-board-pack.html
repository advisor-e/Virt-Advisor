Notes to the forecast

Three-Way Forecast from 1 April 2024

Note 1 · Basis of preparation

These notes explain how this forecast's figures are worked out, which accounting standard governs each treatment, and where the forecast differs from full NZ IFRS.

PURPOSE OF THE FORECAST

This forecast has been prepared to assess the future performance of the business and to support an application for refinancing. It may not be suitable for other purposes.

BASIS OF ASSUMPTIONS

The forecast has been prepared on the basis of assumptions about future events that the directors reasonably expect to occur, and the actions the directors reasonably expect to take.

ACCOUNTING POLICIES

The forecast has been prepared using the accounting policies the business expects to use in its own financial statements for the period, except where Note 5 says otherwise.

The measurement basis is forecast cost. Accrual accounting is used to match costs and revenues.

No material change in accounting policies is expected during the period of the forecast.
